Task

This experiment has 10 rounds. In each round you will observe the outcomes of two assets, Asset A and Asset B. These assets will generate different outcomes depending on the situation, or state, that they are in. For example, take a risky asset like an Apple stock. In some situations, like when the economy is doing really well and people are buying computers, the price of Apple will go up. In other situations, like when the economy is in a recession, the price of Apple will go down. Similarly, if a rival company releases a nice new product, Apple will go down, but if a rival company goes bankrupt, the price of Apple will go up.
You will be provided with information on how well the two different assets do across a number of situations. In some situations one asset will generate a higher outcome than the other, or vice versa. For example, take Asset A and B. In Situation 1, Asset A will generate an outcome of $7 and Asset B will generate an outcome of $2. Alternatively, in Situation 2, Asset A will generate an outcome of $2 and Asset B will generate an outcome of $7. The situation clearly determines which of the two assets is better at any given time. Each situation has an equal chance of being picked at any given time.

The outcomes of Asset A and Asset B in one round are unrelated to the outcomes in other rounds.
{{ if player.participant.incentive == "choice" }} Your task is to choose between the two assets in each round. We will also ask a few additional questions after the rounds. {{ else }} Your task is to guess different outcomes for the two assets in each round. We will also ask a few additional questions after the rounds. {{ endif }}

On the next page the compensation scheme is described.

Bonus Payment Details

{{ if player.participant.incentive == "choice" }}

For each choice, we take all possible outcomes for both assets and rescale them so that the worst outcome across all situations and both assets corresponds to a bonus of £0.00, the best outcome corresponds to a bonus of £2.00, and intermediate outcomes scale up/down with a constant factor.

{{ if player.participant.stakes == "high" }}

For example, if the minimum outcome across all situations is -£1 000 for Asset A in Situation 1, and the maximum outcome across all situations is +£1 000 for Asset B in Situation 2, we rescale outcomes using the formula:
Bonus = (Outcome + £1 000) / 1 000

Example outcome Outcome Bonus
Worst outcome (Asset A, Situation 1) -£1 000 £0.00
An intermediate outcome +£500 £1.50
Best outcome (Asset B, Situation 2) +£1 000 £2.00
{{ else }}

We check how accurate your selected guess is against the statistically correct answer, and rescale that accuracy into a bonus of £0.00 (inaccurate) or £2.00 (accurate).

For guesses where you estimate a outcome (in £) or a likelihood (in %), you earn a bonus of £2.00 if your estimate is within 5% of the correct answer, and £0.00 otherwise.
